NFC Business Touchpoint

Combining a fabricated enclosure with an NFC tag and a useful digital destination.

01 / Engineering challenge

The problem to solve.

A physical business accessory needs to open a useful destination while remaining easy to carry and recognize.

Constraints

  • Tag placement and reading orientation
  • Enclosure thickness
  • Clear digital destination
  • Repeatable fabrication and finish

02 / Engineering approach

The reasoning.

Treat the enclosure, tag and linked page as one interaction. Test reading behavior before standardizing a production design.

03 / Development

What exists today.

Early keychain design and NFC experiments inform the current made-to-order catalog. Catalog visuals illustrate the offering and are not test evidence.

04 / Testing & validation

What the evidence supports.

No universal reading range or cross-device success rate is claimed. A phone-by-phone reading test and a durability review are still needed.

05 / Outcome

Current result. Next experiment.

A prototype direction connected to an existing made-to-order product offering.

  1. Record tag position and wall thickness
  2. Test multiple phones and orientations
  3. Check finish and mechanical durability
  4. Document a repeatable build configuration

Project → Professional conversation

A summary for LinkedIn

An NFC keychain is a hardware-and-interface problem: enclosure thickness, tag placement and the linked page all affect the experience. My next step is documenting repeatable reading tests across devices.
