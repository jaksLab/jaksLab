01 / Engineering challenge

The problem to solve.

A sensor output needs a suitable interface before it can be interpreted by a controller or acquisition system.

Constraints

  • Signal range and supply requirements
  • Component availability
  • A testable circuit and clear measurements

02 / Engineering approach

The reasoning.

Define the signal requirements, compare suitable components and plan bench measurements before concluding that a circuit works.

03 / Development

What exists today.

The work currently focuses on component research, interfaces and a practical test plan. No completed or calibrated instrument is claimed.

04 / Testing & validation

What the evidence supports.

Measured performance is pending. Datasheet expectations are not presented as measurements of an assembled device.

Current result. Next experiment.
