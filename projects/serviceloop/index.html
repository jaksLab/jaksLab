JAKS ServiceLoop

An NFC and QR service touchpoint concept for independent automotive workshops.

01 / Engineering challenge

The problem to solve.

A workshop needs a simple way for a customer to revisit service information and contact the business after a visit.

Constraints

  • Keep workshop updates simple
  • Protect customer and vehicle information
  • Make the link useful without installing an app
  • Validate a real workshop workflow before scaling

02 / Engineering approach

The reasoning.

Explore a customer-facing service page linked from an NFC tag or QR code, with a separate workflow for updating workshop information.

03 / Development

What exists today.

Early web-interface work and feature definition cover service dates, mileage, contact actions and possible loyalty features. The full operational workflow and access controls still need validation.

04 / Testing & validation

What the evidence supports.

No live customer adoption, retention uplift or recurring revenue is claimed. Authentication, data handling and workshop testing remain open work.

05 / Outcome

Current result. Next experiment.

An independent product direction under development, not a released subscription platform.

  1. Interview a workshop owner
  2. Choose one service follow-up workflow
  3. Validate staff access and data handling
  4. Run a limited pilot with explicit consent

Project → Professional conversation

A summary for LinkedIn

JAKS ServiceLoop explores how an NFC or QR touchpoint could keep workshop service information accessible. It is in development; the next milestone is a small workshop workflow test, not a revenue claim.
