JAKS Auto-Buddy

A research concept for keeping vehicle events and maintenance reminders in one place.

01 / Engineering challenge

The problem to solve.

Explore whether a focused physical companion could make vehicle maintenance information easier to capture and revisit.

Constraints

  • A useful first use case
  • Driver distraction and placement
  • Privacy of recorded information
  • Hardware cost and ongoing support

02 / Engineering approach

The reasoning.

Validate the information workflow before committing to cameras, a dedicated device or a possible vehicle-data connection.

03 / Development

What exists today.

Concept and research only. A camera, AI assistant and possible OBD-II connection are options to evaluate, not implemented features.

04 / Testing & validation

What the evidence supports.

No working device, vehicle compatibility, diagnostic capability or commercial availability is claimed.

05 / Outcome

Current result. Next experiment.

A concept in the research backlog.

  1. Interview potential users
  2. Choose one problem to test
  3. Try a simple software-first workflow
  4. Review whether hardware is justified

A summary for LinkedIn

JAKS Auto-Buddy is an early concept for organizing vehicle events and maintenance reminders. Before adding hardware, the goal is to validate whether one focused information workflow is useful.
