Smart Home Retrofit Automation

Giving existing equipment automatic operation and device-based control, instead of replacing the entire product.

01 / Engineering challenge

The problem to solve.

Existing household equipment has useful hardware but lacks the automatic operation or connected control needed by its user.

Constraints

  • Clear input and output behavior
  • Defined operating conditions
  • Documented manual control and failure behavior

02 / Engineering approach

The reasoning.

Reuse the existing product and add a suitable sensing, control and device-interface layer. Document the original behavior, the retrofit and the resulting automatic sequence.

03 / Development

What exists today.

Jonathan has developed home automation by reusing products without their own automatic control and making them operate automatically from a device. His independent experience also includes ESP32, Raspberry Pi and Home Assistant.

04 / Testing & validation

What the evidence supports.

Individual retrofit examples, wiring documentation, operating conditions and test observations will be added to this record. No universal compatibility or certified product status is implied.

05 / Outcome

Current result. Next experiment.

Hands-on retrofit experience connecting existing equipment with automatic operation and a device interface.

  1. Choose one completed retrofit to document
  2. Show original and modified behavior
  3. Record the controller, interface and automatic sequence
  4. Add photos and functional test observations
