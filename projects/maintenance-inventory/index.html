Maintenance Inventory System

A spreadsheet-based approach to finding parts, seeing stock and organizing purchase information.

InventorySearchPurchase workflow

Record prepared
October 30, 2026.
Project start date not recorded.

01 / Engineering challenge

The problem to solve.

A maintenance team needs to find parts by manufacturer, description or exact code while retaining quantity and location context.

Constraints

  • Useful general and exact-code search
  • Keep descriptions, locations and quantities visible
  • Make updates understandable to the person maintaining the file

02 / Engineering approach

The reasoning.

Organize a master parts list and connect it to search, stock views and purchase-order workflows.

03 / Development

What exists today.

A workbook prototype has been developed around a master list, broader part searches and purchase information. Public examples must use synthetic, non-company data.

04 / Testing & validation

What the evidence supports.

No quantified stock reduction or purchasing improvement is claimed. A demonstration dataset and repeatable user tasks are the next validation step.

05 / Outcome

Current result. Next experiment.

A workflow prototype that can be demonstrated with non-confidential sample data.

  1. Prepare a synthetic demonstration dataset
  2. Test broad manufacturer search and exact part lookup
  3. Document the stock-update workflow
  4. Record usability findings

Project → Professional conversation

A summary for LinkedIn

I developed a maintenance inventory workbook prototype focused on practical part lookup: broad manufacturer searches, exact codes, quantities and locations. The next step is a repeatable usability test with synthetic data.
