Design the tap and the destination.

A useful NFC product combines the physical object with the page someone reaches after the tap.

JT
Jonathan TapiaOctober 30, 2026 · JAKS Engineering Lab

The object is one part of the interaction

A custom enclosure gives a physical touchpoint its shape and identity. The page behind the link gives the interaction its purpose: contact a business, revisit information or find a next action.

Choose one action

A focused destination is easier to explain and evaluate. A workshop experiment might begin with contact and service follow-up information before adding points, referrals or other features.

Validate the workflow

The business also needs a practical way to keep information current. That update workflow, appropriate access and consent need to be addressed before treating an early web page as a released service.
