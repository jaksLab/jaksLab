Research brief · 2 min

Capture first. Review before reporting.

The first design question for an AI maintenance tool is the reporting workflow, before the device.

JT
Jonathan TapiaOctober 30, 2026 · JAKS Engineering Lab

The problem to investigate

A maintenance record should preserve what was observed, what was changed and what happened afterward. Capturing that information while doing hands-on work is the problem behind the AI Maintenance Copilot concept.

A smaller first experiment

The proposed first workflow is simple: capture a note, produce a structured draft, review it and export the accepted record. A dedicated camera or wearable is a later decision.

Review is part of the product

A draft should keep unknowns visible. It should not turn an operator report into a measurement, a hypothesis into a cause, or an untested replacement into a successful repair. The person responsible for the record reviews those distinctions.

What would count as progress

A useful early test would compare the same intervention recorded manually and through the proposed workflow. Editing effort, missing information and clarity would guide the next iteration. These are proposed measures; results have not yet been collected.
