Privacy & analytics

Last updated November 17, 2026.

Contact requests

Our inquiry form drafts a message on your device. It does not submit that message to a database. You choose whether to send it using your email application or WhatsApp, which process it according to their own policies. Do not send credentials, confidential drawings or sensitive personal information in initial inquiries.

Optional analytics

JAKS may use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and general clicks to product pages and contact options. Analytics remain disabled until the site owner configures a real measurement property. When configured, tracking starts only after you opt in on this browser. We do not intentionally send form text, names, phone numbers or other message content to analytics. Declining analytics does not prevent you from browsing or contacting us.

You can reset the preference on this browser below and select a new choice. This does not delete information already transmitted with past permission.

External platforms

When you open a link to email, WhatsApp, Etsy, LinkedIn or another provider, that provider's privacy terms apply.
